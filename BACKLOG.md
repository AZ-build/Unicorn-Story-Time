# Backlog — Unicorn Story Time

A parking lot for ideas, features, and questions we want to revisit — but aren't working
on right now.

---

## Feature Ideas

- [ ] **Read-aloud mode** — browser speech synthesis reads a paragraph on demand. Free to
      build, but risks becoming a crutch. Consider limiting it to single hard words only.
- [ ] **Her own chapter** — a simple mode where she writes a choice and where it leads.
      Turning a reader into an author is the single biggest engagement jump available.
- [ ] **Unicorn friends / companions** — collectible characters that join her and comment
      on choices, each with a personality
- [ ] **Photo mode** — pose her decorated unicorn against a chapter background and save it
      as an image she can send to Grandma
- [ ] **Sound and music** — gentle chimes on rewards, ambient chapter themes
- [ ] **Secret chapter** — unlocks only when every treasure in Chapters 1–3 is found
- [ ] **A map screen** — visual world map showing the places she's been
- [ ] **Word Chest quiz** — optional, opt-in, never blocking. Earn a rare cosmetic.
- [ ] **Two-player mode** — Dad reads one character's lines out loud

---

## Questions to Explore

- [ ] Is 10–15 minutes actually the right chapter length for her, or does she want more?
- [ ] Does she prefer replaying chapters to see other paths, or always moving forward?
- [ ] Do the hidden traits feel meaningful to her, or should they be visible?
- [ ] Should there be any consequence at all for a "wrong" choice, or is zero-stakes right?
- [ ] Is she more motivated by cosmetics or by treasures? Lean harder into whichever wins.

---

## Someday / Maybe

- [ ] A second story world with a different creature (dragons? sea horses?) sharing the
      same engine — proves the engine is reusable
- [ ] Let other kids play it — share the link with her friends
- [ ] A simple visual story editor so Adrian (or she) can add chapters without editing code
- [ ] Difficulty that adapts: text gets harder as she progresses through chapters

---

## Deprioritized (decided against for now)

| Idea | Why we set it aside |
|------|---------------------|
| Daily streaks / login rewards | Turns reading-for-joy into an obligation. Wrong for a 7-year-old. |
| Timers or reading-speed scores | Adds anxiety, punishes careful reading |
| "Game over" / losing states | Fear of failure is the fastest way to kill reading momentum |
| Accounts and cloud sync | Adds a privacy surface and complexity for zero benefit at this scale |
| Native iOS/Android app | A PWA installs to the home screen and looks identical. Not worth the cost. |
| In-app purchases of any kind | Obviously not. Noted so it's never revisited. |
